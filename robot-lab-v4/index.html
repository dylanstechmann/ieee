    Assumptions, controls and evidence boundary

    Controls

    Space play/pause · R reset · ←/→ one second · drag orbit · wheel zoom. Toggle wiring, labels or X-ray. Field scrubbing deterministically re-simulates from the initial pose at 120 Hz.

    What the upgrade adds

    Beveled procedural hardware, metallic/roughness materials, soft shadow mapping, rotating wheels, detailed connectors and smooth cable meshes. Electrical telemetry and cable activity derive from the same fixed-step drive state.

    What remains unproven

    No vendor-certified models, released BOM, fabrication drawing, calibrated motor/battery model, wire or fuse sizing, 3D contacts, wheel slip, sensor simulation, mechanism validation, legal route or complete mission.

    Before ordering

    Complete E00 inventory/policy input, measure the actual tire and drivetrain loads, verify candidate datasheets and mechanical drawings, then review power isolation, grounding, connector ratings and wire sizes.
