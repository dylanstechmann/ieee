Model and evidence boundary

Planar approximation. Longitudinal/yaw dynamics switch between a no-slip constraint and independent traction caps. No suspension, lateral tire model, flexible tire, impacts or 3D rigid-body contact is claimed.

Synthetic parameters. Mass, inertia, friction, caster scrub, supply resistance, current limit and controller gains are assumptions. The linear motor approximation is not a calibrated driver or thermal model.

Geometry proxies. Passive fork and reserved electronics volumes are conceptual envelopes, not selected mechanisms or vendor CAD. Exploded mode hides leads and envelopes to avoid false connections.

No physical release. No supplier order, fabrication, setup approval or physical trial is implied. Seeded sensitivity cases are scenario coverage, not measured probability or reliability.
