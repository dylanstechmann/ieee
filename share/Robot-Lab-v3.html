    Assumptions, controls and evidence boundary

    Controls

    Space play/pause · R reset · ←/→ one second · drag orbit · wheel zoom. Follow keeps the simulated robot centered. Field scrubbing re-simulates from the initial pose at the same fixed time step.

    What this does not prove

    No manufacturing readiness, selected BOM, legal route, complete mission, reliable tire exchange, continuous collision proof, sensor performance or physical score. The north route crosses the illustrated start area and remains experimental.

    Sources

    Existing constrained-field geometry, connected-navigation empty-control routes and official tire proportions. Robot parts are visualization concepts. The reviewed organizer questions are saved separately in confirmation-questions-v3.json.
