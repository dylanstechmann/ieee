## Checks (arithmetic on mule-v10 geometry)

- **Overall wheel width:** 212 mm, inside the 254 mm base. Wheel slots leave a 18 mm outer ligament on the square plate.
- **Round plate:** the slot corners reach 117.175 mm from center against the 127 mm radius, so the slots fit inside.
- **Plate height:** 23.6–28.6 mm above the floor, inside the 76.2 mm band where R01 requires at least 10 x 10 in.
