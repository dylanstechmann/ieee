## Compatibility notes

- #1995 brackets include motor and bracket mounting screws; #1084 stamped brackets omit chassis-side screws.
- #1083 hubs fit the 6 mm shafts and include set screws. Wheel mounting screws are separate.
- #1430 wheels are 80 mm, not the 3-inch baseline. Their silicone tires are a mule traction candidate, not a measured field match.
- Each #953 caster includes two #2 screws and nuts plus spacers; separate 25-packs were removed from required Order A. Final deck height, one-versus-two-caster support and wheel loading require measurement.
- Pololu warns not to screw more than 3.5 mm into the motor mounting holes; verify actual bracket and screw engagement.
