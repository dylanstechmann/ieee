## B. Obtain dimensioned geometry before acquiring a sample

Ask organizers for installed vehicle models or conservative official envelopes,
allowed substitution/orientation, placement datum and tolerances. Manufacturer
specifications can corroborate a model identity but do not establish the organizer's
chosen specimens, mounting or permissible substitutions. Package dimensions are
not vehicle footprint dimensions. No purchase is authorized by this checklist.

If an approved representative specimen or field becomes available, request:

| Record | Required observation |
|---|---|
| Provenance | Specimen/model identity, who measured, date, organizer approval and applicability to event |
| Vehicle plan envelope | Maximum length and width including wheels, bumpers and protrusions, not just body shell |
| Height / shape | Highest feature and plan footprint at possible contact/sensor heights; bounding rectangle or measured polygon |
| Vehicle variation | Each possible vehicle type and differences between actual specimens; no extrapolation from one toy to entire set |
| Orientation | Allowed yaw range, whether staff can reverse/rotate vehicles, any permitted motion after placement |
| Field datum | Origin, inside board/rail faces, both divider endpoints, rail installed widths/heights and tower projections |
| Placement | Region corners, full-object containment versus reference point, allocation of three cars, orientation and tolerance |
| Instruments | Ruler/caliper identifier or resolution, repeated readings, units and reasonable measurement uncertainty |
| Photos | Top-down and side views, ruler in the object's plane, datum marks and unit display visible; perspective pictures only corroborate |

The owner/organizer supplies physical observations. Label agent-extracted image
measurements separately; never claim the owner measured or reviewed them when not true.
Do not substitute a measurement instrument's resolution for whole-field tolerance.

## C. Promote evidence to the model deliberately

For each Q ID, attach source/answer → interpretation → affected parameter/policy →
required tests → independent review state. Keep confirmed dimensions, drawing
interpretations, engineering assumptions and unknowns in distinct fields.

- Q1–Q3: source-backed access/containment/lap state machine; extra/reverse crossing
  and start-area traversal tests, then a complete three-lap mission specification.
- Q4–Q6: admissible **three-vehicle** configurations with bounded orientation,
  clearances and placement tolerances; preserve square probes as regression fixtures.
- Q7: separate exchange reach from final parking; model actual chassis, held tire
  and mechanism swept envelope before freeing conservative docking poses.

An answer that prohibits north access retires it from legal candidate comparisons
without deleting its historical counterfactual result. Unanswered rules remain
separately named hypotheses. Do not reduce uncertainty to make a route pass.

## D. Validate after—not instead of—confirmation

Rerun complete arrival/task/withdrawal/reentry checks for all assigned boxes and
allowed obstacle configurations. Independently check physical interval clearance,
contract progress, carried-object/mechanism envelope and docking. Retain failures
and untested searches. Then plan separate low-speed physical tests with emergency
stop, measured installed field and recorded outcomes; no such trial occurred here.

Finite enumerated success is neither continuous adversarial coverage nor control,
sensing, traction, dynamics or competition qualification.
